# My Favorite Series

## Moon Lovers: Scarlet Heart Ryeo

**Moon Lovers: Scarlet Heart Ryeo** is a South Korean historical fantasy drama released in *2016*. It is based on a Chinese novel and set in the Goryeo dynasty.

## Story Summary
A woman from modern times is suddenly transported to the Goryeo era, where she wakes up in the body of a young noblewoman named **Hae Soo**. She gets caught up in the lives of the royal princes and the struggle for the throne.

## Main Characters
- **Hae Soo** (IU)
- **Wang So**, the 4th Prince (Lee Joon-gi)
- **Wang Wook**, the 8th Prince (Kang Ha-neul)
- **Wang Yo**, the 3rd Prince (Hong Jong-hyun)

## Why I Like It
1. Emotional and touching story
2. Beautiful costumes and cinematography
3. Great acting and unforgettable soundtrack

## Favorite Line or Scene
> The scene where **Wang So**, the 4th Prince, finally learns about **Hae Soo's** death and cries heavily while carrying her ashes.
This scene is my favorite because it shows how deeply he loved her. It was *heartbreaking* and one of the most emotional moments of the series.
